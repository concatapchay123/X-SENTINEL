### Audit Findings & Traceability Summary

1. **Foundational Consistency (BE-01 to BE-04)**: The data layer enforces exact 2,381 feature vectors, rejects NaN/Inf/booleans, and splits vectors into 3 distinct semantic views.
2. **Authoritative Science vs Advisory AI (BE-05 to BE-13)**: The core detection pipeline (LightGBM + TreeSHAP + M1–M5 + Fusion) operates with zero runtime dependency on Jev AI or external network calls. Jev AI operates exclusively in an advisory capacity with strict single-token logprob decision contracts and prompt injection defenses.
3. **Research Integrity (BE-14)**: Software implementation and candidate experimental runs are 100% complete and reproducible. Strict scientific sign-off is responsibly blocked pending resolution of OQ-01, OQ-02, and availability of pre-trained poisoned LightGBM models.
4. **Security & Deployment (BE-15 to BE-19)**: Zero hardcoded secrets, verified RBAC, non-root Docker builds, single-head Alembic migrations, and fail-closed Raw PE ingestion provide comprehensive defense-in-depth.
